Orbit Connect uses EasyTier and Terracotta as separate, unmodified applications.
Native applications are downloaded directly from their upstream release pages on first use.
No EasyTier, Terracotta, Npcap/Packet.dll or Microsoft runtime binary is embedded in OrbitLauncher.exe.
Network drivers are not installed. EasyTier runs with no_tun=true.
